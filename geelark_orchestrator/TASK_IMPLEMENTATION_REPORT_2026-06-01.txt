    def _verify_google_account(phone_id: str) -> tuple[bool, str]:
        """
        Runtime Google account verification.
        Runs 'dumpsys account | grep "Account {"' to confirm at least one
        Google account exists on the phone.
        Returns (True, account_email) or (False, reason).
        """
        try:
            r = _post(
                "/open/v1/shell/execute",
                {"id": phone_id, "cmd": 'dumpsys account | grep "Account {"'},
            )
            out = r.get("output", "") or ""
            for line in out.splitlines():
                if "Account {" in line and "type=com.google" in line:
                    m = re.search(r'name=([^,]+)', line)
                    if m:
                        return True, m.group(1)
            if out.strip():
                return False, f"dumpsys returned non-Google accounts: {out.strip()[:200]}"
            return False, "no accounts found in dumpsys"
        except Exception as e:
            return False, str(e)

    - Click "Directions" with 8000ms search time
    - Wait 3000ms
    - Click "Call" with 8000ms search time (fallback)
    - Wait 3000ms
    - VERIFY: wait_for_element checks for text "Start"

    - Wait 2000ms

TASK 3: Business Confirmation After Selection
================================================================================

FILE MODIFIED: geelark_orchestrator/scripts/run_full_maps_flow.py

ADDED AFTER the scroll-and-find business click section:

    # ---- Stage 3b: Business confirmation ----
    fb.wait_for_element("text", profile.business_name,
                        search_time_ms=8000,
                        remark="VERIFY: business card title matches expected")
    fb.wait(2000, "Wait after business confirmation")

WHAT IT DOES:
    After clicking the business name in search results, the flow waits up to
    8 seconds for the expected business_name text to appear as a header/title
    element on the opened card. If the text is not found (wrong business opened
    or click failed), the wait_for_element step times out. With error_type="skip"
    the flow continues, but the post-run evidence will show business_found=False.

LIMITATION:
    The Geelark flow builder uses error_type="skip" globally, so a failed
    wait_for_element does not abort the flow. The verification is retrospective
    — the evidence collector (maps_evidence.py) checks if the business name
    appears on the final screen. A future enhancement would use error_type="stop"
    for the business confirmation step to hard-abort on mismatch.
